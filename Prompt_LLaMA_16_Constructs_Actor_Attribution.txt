• UTAUT-III
• Perceived Risk Theory
• Attribution Theory

The objective is to transform unstructured customer reviews into structured measures representing:

A. Service Quality Dimensions
B. Operational Risk Dimensions
C. Customer Experience Dimensions

SERVICE QUALITY

1. PerformanceExpectancy

2. EffortExpectancy
